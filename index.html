Scene 01 · The first time I saw you

I was wearing a red shirt. You were walking towards me in a mouse-coloured dress.

And, for a moment, my brain decided this was no ordinary entrance. Somewhere in my imagination, the violins had started playing like a scene from Main Hoon Na—Sushmita Sen making that iconic entrance, a dramatic breeze blowing, and everyone around us inexplicably dancing in the background.

None of that actually happened.
Except, somehow, all of it did in my head.

There was no cinematic wind machine. No background dancers. No orchestra hiding behind a tree. Just you walking towards me—and me, apparently, directing an entire romantic blockbuster internally.
